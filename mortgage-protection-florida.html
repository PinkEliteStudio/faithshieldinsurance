What is mortgage protection insurance?

Mortgage protection is life insurance built around your home loan. When something happens to you, the policy pays your family a tax-free benefit. They can pay off the house, keep making the payments, or use the money for whatever they need most. The money goes to your family, not the bank.

PMI vs. mortgage protection

Most Florida homeowners believe the PMI on their mortgage protects their family. It doesn't. Here's the difference:

PMIMortgage protection
Who it protectsThe lenderYour family
Who gets paidThe bankYour spouse, children or beneficiary
When you refinance or sellIt endsIt stays with you for the full term
Living benefitsNoneMany plans pay early for a qualifying critical, chronic or terminal illness

Why it matters for Florida families

  • Most families could cover only a few months of mortgage payments when a paycheck stops.
  • Florida home values and insurance costs keep rising, so the house is often a family's biggest asset and biggest bill.
  • When the person paying for the house passes, grief and the fear of losing the home arrive at the same time. No family should carry both.

How much does it cost?

Your price depends on your age, health, tobacco use and how much coverage you choose. For many Florida families, protecting their home costs less than their monthly coffee budget. Many plans have no medical exam, and some families are approved the same day.

Questions Florida homeowners ask

Is PMI the same as mortgage protection?

No. PMI protects the lender when a loan defaults. Mortgage protection is life insurance that pays your family so they can keep the home.

Do I need a medical exam?

Many plans have no medical exam. You answer health questions by phone, and some families are approved the same day.

What happens to my coverage when I refinance or sell?

The policy belongs to you, not the bank, so it keeps protecting your family for the full term after you refinance, sell or pay off the home.
