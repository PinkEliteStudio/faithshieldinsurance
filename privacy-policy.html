We collect information you voluntarily provide when you fill out a form on this Site, including our free guide request, quote request, newsletter sign-up forms, and the website chat assistant. Depending on which form you submit, this may include:

  • Full name (first and last)

We may also automatically collect limited technical information, such as your general location, browser type, and how you interact with the Site, through standard hosting and analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Prepare and discuss personalized life insurance, mortgage protection, disability income, annuity, or related quotes
  • Contact you by phone, text message, or email regarding your inquiry, quote, or appointment
  • Deliver the free Family Protection Guide and send our monthly Faith & Finance newsletter, if requested
  • Schedule calls or appointments you book through Calendly
  • Maintain records as required by Florida insurance regulations and our licensing carriers
  • Improve the Site and our communications with you

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Consent to Be Contacted (Phone, Text & Email)

When you request a quote, I will contact you about that request by phone or email. Our quote form also has two optional checkboxes, which are never pre-checked and are not required to get a quote:

  • Text messages: if you check this box, you agree to receive text messages from FaithShield Insurance about your quote request, appointment scheduling and reminders, and follow-up on your coverage questions. Up to 4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. See our Text Messaging Terms.
  • Calls: if you check this box, you agree that Rhina Delgado of FaithShield Insurance may call you about your quote, including voicemail messages that may be prerecorded.

Consent is not a condition of purchasing any insurance product. You may opt out of texts by replying STOP, unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email, and ask to stop phone calls at any time by telling us directly.

I own and operate my own private client system (CRM). When you submit a quote request, use the chat assistant, or book a call, your information goes into the system I built and own. It is not a shared or rented CRM, and no other company uses it.

Your information is never sold, rented, traded, or shared with any third party for marketing.

To run my website and system, a few service providers store or deliver information only on my behalf, under strict security, and are not allowed to use it for anything else:

  • Secure hosting and database providers (such as Netlify and Supabase), which host this website and store my private system
