Freedom question

Does My Life Insurance Through Work Cover My Mortgage?

Coverage through work belongs to the job. When the job changes, so does your family's protection.

See My Options in 2 Minutes
Short answer: usually not fully. Group life insurance through an employer is often one or two times your yearly salary, while the mortgage alone can be several times that. And the coverage is tied to the job: when you leave, retire or get laid off, it ends or has to be converted, often at a higher price. Coverage you own goes wherever you go.

Do the math on your own house

Take your yearly salary and multiply it by what your benefits page says (often 1x or 2x). Now compare it to your mortgage balance. For most Florida homeowners, the work policy covers part of the house and none of the other bills: car, childcare, groceries, college.

What happens when you leave the job

  • Coverage usually ends when you leave, retire or are let go.
  • Some plans let you convert or port it, often with a short deadline and higher rates, because now you pay the full price yourself.
  • Your health may have changed. Buying new coverage later can cost more, or be harder to get, than buying it while you are young and healthy.

The freedom of owning your coverage

Coverage you own is yours: you choose the amount, you choose who it protects, and it stays the same when you change jobs, start a business or retire early. Your family's protection should never depend on your boss.

Many families keep the work policy as a bonus and own a policy built around the mortgage. That way the house is covered no matter where life takes you.

Questions families ask

Is work life insurance enough?

For most homeowners it covers part of the mortgage and none of the other bills. It also stays with the job, so it can disappear when you need it most.

Can I keep my work policy when I leave?

Some plans let you convert or port it, usually within a short window and at a higher cost. Check your benefits booklet for the exact deadline.

I'm self-employed. What can I do?

You get to build your own protection from the start: coverage you own, sized to your mortgage and your family, that no job change can take away.
