Short answer: start with your mortgage balance, add a cushion of several months of household bills, and match the length of the coverage to the years left on your loan. The calculator below gives you a starting number in 30 seconds.

Your starting number

How the number works

  1. Mortgage balance: what it takes to own the house outright.
  2. A cushion for bills: months of household costs while your family adjusts. Many families choose 6 to 12 months.
  3. Final costs: a funeral in Florida can cost thousands of dollars.
  4. Minus coverage you already own: a work policy counts only while you keep that job.

How long should the coverage last?

Match the term to the years left on your mortgage. A 30-year loan with 27 years left usually pairs with a 25 or 30-year term. Some families add coverage that lasts a lifetime, so something is always left for the children.

Questions families ask

Should the coverage equal my mortgage balance exactly?

That is the minimum most families start with. A cushion for bills gives your family time to grieve instead of rushing to sell.

Does the coverage go down as I pay the mortgage?

Not with a policy you own. Many term plans stay level, so the money left after the house is paid goes to your family.

Do I need a medical exam?

Many plans have no medical exam. You answer health questions by phone, and some families are approved the same day.
