Health questions

Can I Get Mortgage Protection With Health Problems?

Most people stop at the health questions because they aren't sure what counts. Here is what really matters.

See My Options in 2 Minutes
Short answer: often, yes. Many Florida families with high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, past smoking or extra weight still qualify. Each insurance company looks at health differently, so the same person can be declined by one and approved by another. That is why working with a broker who compares many carriers matters.

What insurance companies look at

  • Your age and build: height and weight together.
  • Tobacco or nicotine: including vaping, and how long ago you quit.
  • Health conditions: what they are, when they started, and how well they are controlled.
  • Medications: they tell the carrier how a condition is managed.

Why one "no" is not the final answer

Every carrier has its own rules. One company may charge more for well-controlled diabetes, while another treats it kindly. A captive agent can only offer one company. A broker compares many and matches your health to the carrier that fits you best.

Options for almost every family

  • No-exam plans: health questions by phone, with some approvals the same day.
  • Simplified plans: fewer questions for people with more health history.
  • Guaranteed acceptance plans: for people who can't qualify elsewhere, usually with a waiting period at the start.

Be honest on the application. Wrong answers can cause a claim to be denied, which is the one thing a family should never face.

Questions families ask

Will one decline hurt my chances with other carriers?

Each carrier makes its own decision. A broker can usually find another company that looks at your health differently.

I smoked years ago. Do I pay smoker rates?

Many carriers give non-smoker rates after you have been tobacco-free for a set time, often 12 months or more. The rules vary by company.

Do I need medical records?

Most no-exam plans only need your answers by phone. The carrier checks prescription and medical databases with your permission.
